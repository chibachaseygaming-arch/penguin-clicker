PENGUIN CLICKER APP

IN THIS APP ZIP

- index.html: the complete game. Double-click this file to play offline in your
  browser; game progress is saved in that browser.
- manifest.webmanifest, service-worker.js, and icons: files needed to install
  the game as an app and play offline.

INSTALL THE APP

The easiest way is to open this game at:
https://chibachaseygaming-arch.github.io/penguin-clicker/

Then use your browser menu:
- Chrome or Edge: choose "Install Penguin Clicker" or "Install app".
- iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

The files in this ZIP can also be hosted on any HTTPS website to install the
app and enable offline play. Opening index.html directly still lets you play
offline without installing the app.
